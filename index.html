Frequently Asked Questions

What is Books Timothy / PJC Songs?
Books Timothy (also known as PJC Songs) is a digital library app offering spiritual books, counseling resources, leadership college content, and an e-library — developed by Dr. Timothy Aryal and Samuel Thapa Magar.
How do I access all books?
Create an account and log in. Once logged in, you will have access to the full e-library including spiritual growth books, counseling resources, Bible study materials, and more.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login screen, tap Forgot Password and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address. If you continue to have trouble, contact us at samuelthapamagar77@gmail.com.
How do I update my profile information?
Go to the Profile tab at the bottom of the app and tap Edit Profile to update your name, email, or other details.
The app is not working correctly. What should I do?
Try closing and reopening the app first. If the problem persists, make sure your app is updated to the latest version. For continued issues, email us with a description of the problem and your device model.
