Frequently Asked Questions

What is Books Timothy?
Books Timothy is a digital library app containing spiritual books written by Dr. Timothy Aryal. The app was developed by Samuel Thapa Magar to make Dr. Aryal's books, counseling resources, and leadership college content easily accessible on mobile.
Who wrote the books in the app?
All the books available in the app are authored by Dr. Timothy Aryal. His writings cover spiritual growth, Bible study, counseling, and Christian leadership.
Who developed this app?
The Books Timothy app was developed by Samuel Thapa Magar. For any technical issues or support, you can reach him at samuelthapamagar77@gmail.com.
How do I access all books?
Create an account and log in. Once logged in, you will have full access to Dr. Timothy Aryal's e-library including spiritual growth books, Bible study materials, counseling resources, and more.
Are all books free to read?
Some books are available for free, while certain premium books require a one-time payment to unlock. Premium books are clearly marked in the app. Once you pay the required amount, the book is permanently unlocked for your account. If you have any questions about pricing or payments, feel free to contact us.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the login screen, tap Forgot Password and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address. If you continue to have trouble, contact us at samuelthapamagar77@gmail.com.
How do I update my profile information?
Go to the Profile tab at the bottom of the app and tap Edit Profile to update your name, email, or other details.
The app is not working correctly. What should I do?
Try closing and reopening the app first. If the problem persists, make sure your app is updated to the latest version. For continued issues, email Samuel at samuelthapamagar77@gmail.com with a description of the problem and your device model.
